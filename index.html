About

From Mitrovica
to the Olympics.

I grew up in Sremska Mitrovica and started paddling when I was 12. By 15, I had made Serbia’s national team.

Since then, I’ve raced at World and European Championships, World Cups, and two Olympic Games — Tokyo in 2021 and Paris in 2024. Along the way, I’ve won World and European U23 titles.

My main events are K1 500m and 1000m. They demand speed and endurance in different proportions, so much of my training is about finding the right balance of technique, power and pace.
