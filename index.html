01 / THE ATHLETE

A life measured
in thousandths.

I’m a Serbian canoe sprint athlete competing at the highest level of international sport.

I started paddling at twelve and joined Serbia’s national team at fifteen. Since then, I’ve earned medals at European and World Championships across junior and U23 categories, competed on the senior World Cup circuit, and represented Serbia at the Olympic Games.

Beyond the water, I’m drawn to systems, details, and constant improvement — the same mindset that led me to a degree in Software Engineering.
