THE ATHLETE

A life measured
in seconds.

I’m a Serbian canoe sprint athlete competing at the highest level of international sport.

I started paddling at twelve and earned a place on Serbia’s national team at fifteen. Since then, I’ve progressed from junior and U23 racing to the senior international circuit, winning medals at World and European Championships and lining up against the world’s best at World Cup events. In 2021, I represented Serbia at the Tokyo Olympic Games — a defining milestone built on years of training, travel, and race-day pressure.

Canoe sprint is decided by seconds — sometimes fractions of them. The work happens long before the start: building endurance, refining technique, developing power, and learning to hold pace under pressure. My focus is K1 500m and 1000m, where efficiency, race judgement, and consistency turn preparation into performance.
